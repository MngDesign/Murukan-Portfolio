AI-Assisted Sales Intelligence Dashboard

Role: Senior UX Designer / UX Research
Domain:Data Visualization Design
Tools: AI-assisted prototyping using Figma and OpenAI Codex
What is this product?

Designing a data visualization dashboard that helps sales teams quickly understand performance, trends, and opportunities.

Goal

Transform complex sales data into clear, decision-ready insights.

Problem

Sales managers often struggle with:

  • Too many spreadsheets
  • Difficult-to-interpret reports
  • Delayed insights for decision making

Opportunity

Design a simple analytics dashboard that helps users quickly answer:

  • Which products perform best?
  • Which region is declining?
  • Where should we focus sales effort?

1. UX Strategy

Key UX Principles
1. Data Hierarchy
  • Most important metrics first.
2. Scan-Friendly Layout
  • Users should understand the situation in 5 seconds.
3. Visual Storytelling
    Use appropriate charts for different insights.
  • Revenue trend - Line chart
  • Product comparison - Bar chart
  • Revenue trend - Funnel
  • Regional performance - Map

2. Dashboard Design

Top KPI Cards
  • Total Revenue
  • Sales Growth
  • Active Deals
  • Conversion Rate
Data Visualization
  • Revenue trend line chart
  • Product performance bar chart
  • Regional sales map
  • Sales funnel visualization
AI Insight Panel
Example insight: “ North region sales droppedsales dropped 12% this quarter. Focus on Product B campaigns.”

3. Impact

Business Value
  • Faster decision making
  • Better understanding of sales performance
  • AI-generated recommendations
Design Outcome
A clear, scalable analytics dashboard designed for modern data teams.
Research Conclusion
My goal was not just to visualize data but to design a system that helps users make faster business decisions.

Promt for Figma
Create a modern enterprise analytics dashboard for a Sales Intelligence platform.

Layout: * Left sidebar navigation with icons * Top bar with filters for date, region, and product * Grid layout dashboard

Include the following components:
1. KPI cards showing:
  • Total Revenue
  • Sales Growth
  • Active Deals
  • Conversion Rate
2. Line chart showing revenue trends over time.
3. Bar chart showing sales by product category.
4. Regional performance map showing sales by location.
5. Sales funnel visualization:
  • Leads → Opportunities → Deals → Closed Sales.
6. AI Insight panel with recommendations such as:
  • "North region revenue dropped 12% this quarter."

Design style: Modern enterprise SaaS UI similar to Power BI or Tableau.

Color palette: White background with blue and purple accents.

Typography: Clean and readable with strong hierarchy for data.

Target users: Sales managers and business analysts.
