Improving Telecom Operations Through Better UX

Role: UX Designer
Domain: Telecom Operations
Org: IBM India (AT&T)
What is this product?

This case study covers internal telecom operations tools used for implementation planning, reviews, and operational coordination.

The interface handled high-stakes workflows, where missing information or unclear states created delays and handoff issues.

The brief

The objective was to reduce friction in multi-step flows and improve confidence during critical operational decisions.

I simplified screen hierarchy, tightened action grouping, and improved visibility of status changes across related views.

Outcome

The redesign improved flow readability and reduced confusion during implementation and review tasks, helping teams move faster with fewer rework cycles.
