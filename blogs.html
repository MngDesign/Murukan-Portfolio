Design Harmony
We can ensure design harmony by using Golden ratio and Fibonacci series in our designs
Many of the designs in nature that used Golden ratio and Fibonacci.
By seeing a frame, eyes starts a quick scan from one point and stops at another, then starts to connect the boath.
Thats how visualy we understanding the design (shape) of the objects.

Nature follows mathematical ratios to make design harmony.

The Golden Ratio is one of the natural design phenominas. -

We call it as the Golden Section or Divine Proportion and asigned the value of 1.618 (Greek letter Phi =1.618)

Nature follows this mathematical ratio to build even tiny sea shell to our body and face.
In fact, our brains are programmed, to see the natural beauty with the objects that use the Golden Ratio principles.
There’s a subconscious attraction to the things having Golden Ratio because brain calculates it as true with the nature.
It is a common design factor in Pyramids, Da Vinci’s Mona Lisa, and in the Pepsi logo.
How to make a Golden ratio design
The total width, divided by 1.618 you will get the Golden ratio and ideal height of the shape
Suppose; Width = 1024
1024/1.618 = 633
1024-633 = 391
If the Large part divided by Short part you will get 1.618
633/391 = 1.618
The sum of Large and Short divided by Large part you will get 1.618
(633+391 ) /633 =1.681

Imagine, we have a square shape(A) with x=5 and Y= 5.
Multiply the x value of shape-A with the GR number 1.618 to get an another shape
5 X 1.618 = 8.09
Align the AInside of B
Now you got a rectangle having balanced and harmonies portions
A golden ratio shape has been created!.
If you keep applying the Golden Ratio formula to the new rectangle, you will get progressively (more and more) smaller squares

Case study 2- Great Pyramid of Giza in Egypt.
(Built around 2560 BC.)
The dimensions of the great pyramid not selected randomly, they were based on Golden Ratio- Golden triangle.

Referance: Equation for golden triangle:
The dimensions of the great pyramid of Giza(Egypt) is equaltothe dimensions of the Golden triangle.
Let's see how?
First they decided the bottom width as 230 mtr, but yet to be find the height as the ideal propotion.
The golden ratio triangle base width is 230/2 = 115m
As per the Golden Ratio Triangle, if the Base width is 1 then the slope should be: Base X 1.618 = 1.618
Here our base width is 115 mtr, so the slope length of the pyramid is 115m x1.618 = 186m
 And the hight of the Golden Ratio Triangle is the squareroot of the 1.618 = 1.27
That means, if the basewidth is 1, then the hieght should be: Base X 1.27 (1x1.27)
So here the height of the pyramid is: 115 m x 1.27 = 146m
It is same as 115/115= 1
186/115 = 1.618 (Phi)
146/115 = 1.27 (squareroot of phi)

The interesting question is “why did they choose this specific shape geometry”?
The answer is: Because it appeared more beautiful, more aligned with nature!
