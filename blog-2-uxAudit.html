UX Audit
What is ux audit and why the process start with it?
A UX audit is a structured review of an existing product (or concept) to discover usability problems, business friction, and opportunities for improvement. It’s the factual foundation for everything we design next — decisions, priorities, and measurable goals. Starting with an audit avoids guessing, reduces rework, and focuses design effort where it delivers the most business value.
High-level outcome
After the audit the client receives:
  • A prioritized list of usability issues (with evidence).
  • Impact vs. effort recommendations (what to fix first).
  • UX metrics to measure success.
  • Actionable design and product recommendations (wireframe sketches, navigation fixes, content fixes, analytics events to track).
  • A clear roadmap for the next UX work (e.g., redesign phases, A/B tests, usability testing).
Step-by-step UX audit process
1. Kickoff & alignment
Goal: Understand business context, users, constraints.
What we do:
  • Meet stakeholders (product, engineering, ops, sales, support).
  • Capture business goals, KPI targets, non-negotiable constraints (tech, compliance)
  • Gather existing artifacts: analytics access, user research, support tickets, product roadmap, product flows, personas..
Deliverable: Audit scope document and access checklist.
Step-by-step UX audit process
2. Heuristic / expert review
Goal: Surface obvious usability issues quickly.
What we do:
  • UX experts evaluate screens and flows against usability heuristics (consistency, feedback, error prevention, affordances, recognition over recall, etc.).
  • Document every issue: where, what, why, severity (low/medium/high).
Deliverable: Heuristic issues log with screenshots and short explanations.
Step-by-step UX audit process
3. Analytics & quantitative review
Goal: Find where users struggle at scale.
What we do:
  • Analyze product analytics (funnels, drop-offs, conversion rates, time on task).
  • Look for high-traffic pages with poor conversion or unusual exit rates.
  • Review click heatmaps and session recordings (if available).
Deliverable: Analytics findings with charts and recommended analytics events to add.
Step-by-step UX audit process
4. Qualitative user review (if users available) / Stakeholder interviews
Goal: Hear why users behave the way they do.
What we do:
  • Interview customer support, sales, and a few end users (or review recorded interviews).
  • Run 3–5 guerrilla usability tests on critical tasks (or review past test recordings).
  • Capture verbatim pain points and observed behavior.
Deliverable: User insights summary with quotes and task success/failure notes.
Step-by-step UX audit process
5. Task & flow analysis
Goal: Map critical journeys and identify friction points.
What we do:
  • Define the product’s core tasks/goals (e.g., “create cargo plan”, “validate stowage”).
  • Map user flows step-by-step and annotate friction (confusing labels, missing states).
Deliverable: low maps with annotated issues.
Step-by-step UX audit process
6. Accessibility & performance check
Goal: Ensure product is usable by everyone and performs well.
What we do:
  • Run accessibility checks (contrast, keyboard navigation, ARIA where applicable).
  • Run basic performance checks (page load, large assets that block tasks).
Deliverable: Accessibility & performance checklist and remediation suggestions.
Step-by-step UX audit process
7. Competitive & standards scan
Goal: Benchmark and pick best practices.
What we do:
  • Quick look at 2–3 competitor products or reference systems to identify patterns users expect.
Deliverable: Comparative notes and recommended patterns.
Step-by-step UX audit process
8. Prioritization & roadmap
Goal: Translate findings into action.
What we do:
  • Score each issue by Impact (user/business) and Effort (dev/time).
  • Create a prioritized backlog (Quick wins, Medium ROI, Long-term).
  • Propose measurable success metrics for each item..
Deliverable: Prioritized action backlog + proposed success metrics.
Step-by-step UX audit process
9. Audit report & presentation
Goal: Present findings and agree next steps.
What we do:
  • Produce a concise audit report and a slide deck for stakeholders.
  • Walk through the findings, demonstrate examples, and agree on immediate next actions (e.g., prototype a new flow, quick UI fixes, more user testing).
  • Propose measurable success metrics for each item.
Deliverable: Final audit report, slide deck, raw evidence folder (screenshots, analytics exports, recordings).
Deliverables checklist (what the client gets)
  • Audit scope & access checklist
  • Heuristic issues log (with screenshots)
  • Analytics report summary (key funnels and drop-offs)
  • User insights summary (quotes + task observations)
  • Flow maps annotated with issues
  • Accessibility & performance checklist
  • Prioritized backlog (impact/effort)
  • Recommended success metrics & tracking plan
  • Slide deck + recorded walkthrough session
