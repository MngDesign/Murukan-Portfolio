Case study 3
Tug Planner
Combines real-time visibility, guided tasks, and smart alerts.

Tug Planner empowers you with clear visuals, timely data, and guided steps — so every plan feels simple, smart, and satisfying.

INDUSTRY:
Marine Cargo shipping
CATEGORY:
Responsive Web application
MARKET:
Global
USERS:
Voyage Planners, Port Captains and Operations Coordinators
UI UX ROLE:
Requirement analyse, Sketches, UX Research, Wireframe, Visual designig and Interactin designing.
Challenges

  • The old website lacked User first approah and responsiveness in various devices.

  • Users require a single unified platform to access all necessary information, instead of relying on multiple sources such as emails, spreadsheets, and weather reports.

  • Users also need visual plans, including 2D/3D representations of tier layouts, and the ability to view them live in the real-world context.

    This challenge hindered the company’s ability to build brand loyalty and monetize user engagement. By troubleshooting existing problems and seamlessly adapting to our client’s business requirements, our solution effectively resolved these issues.
Solution
To give shape to our client’s vision, we conducted a series of product workshops to align business goals, user needs, and technical feasibility. Through iterative UX/UI design processes, we transformed conceptual ideas into tangible experiences. The engagement concluded with the delivery of high-fidelity interactive prototypes that accurately reflected the final product vision.

  • First-Class User Experience I focused on creating a clean, intuitive, and user-centered interface. The design ensured seamless navigation, consistent interactions, and visual harmony—enhancing user satisfaction and reducing friction across key user journeys.

  • Increased User Engagement By simplifying workflows, improving task visibility, and introducing interactive data visuals, users could complete actions faster and with more confidence. Gamified micro-interactions and personalized dashboards further encouraged continued engagement.

  • Streamlined Information Flow A unified design system and information architecture made it easier for users to access data from various sources in one place—reducing dependency on emails, spreadsheets, and manual updates.

  • Enhanced Business Alignment The UX strategy was closely tied to business KPIs—supporting brand consistency, operational efficiency, and monetization goals.

  • Scalable Design Framework A modular component library was developed, ensuring scalability and design consistency for future product updates.
Results
The new website has helped the company elevate its digital presence and strengthen customer trust. By aligning user needs with business goals, the redesigned platform delivered measurable improvements across multiple areas.

  • Improved User Engagement: Users now spend significantly more time exploring key sections of the website, thanks to intuitive navigation and clear content hierarchy.

  • Enhanced Brand Perception: The modern, visually cohesive design reinforces brand identity and credibility, leading to higher customer confidence and satisfaction.

  • Higher Conversion and Retention Rates: Streamlined user flows and engaging CTAs have reduced drop-offs and encouraged repeat visits.

  • Operational Efficiency: Centralized information architecture and optimized content management have simplified internal updates and reduced maintenance effort.
